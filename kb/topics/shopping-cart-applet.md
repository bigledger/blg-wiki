# Shopping Cart (Internal)

The back-office applet for `INTERNAL_SHOPPING_CART` generic documents (short code `SHPCRT`): staff create, edit, FINAL and delete carts that also arrive from customer logins (Customer Access applet) and CP Commerce storefronts. A cart carries a customer, branch/location, items and optional payment lines; it posts nothing (0/0 signums) and exists to be knocked off into a Sales Order.

## How it connects

- **internal-shopping-cart-customer-access-applet** — same document type, ecom endpoint, one cart per entity; staff see those carts here.
- **cp-commerce-admin-applet** — storefront checkout/point purchase creates carts; `line_integrity_config` lives on the website row; storefront conversion to Sales Order is backend-side.
- **internal-sales-order-applet** — the only consumer of FINAL carts (v2 Search Shopping Cart / KO panel through the open queue).
- **knock-off-configuration / organisation-applet** — the `INTERNAL_SHOPPING_CART -> INTERNAL_SALES_ORDER` LINE row gates both the queue rows and the Sales Order panel.
- **internal-receipt-voucher-applet** — payment lines are RV-shaped; the cart-to-RV conversion is unreachable code.
- **cashbook-applet** — settlement methods (`SETTLEMENT_TYPE`) drive the Add Payment dialog.
- **customer-maintenance-applet** — embedded customer editor writes the same entity records.
