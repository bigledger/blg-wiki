# Consignment Purchase Order (Internal)

The order you send a consignor asking them to place stock with you. It is a request, not a purchase: the engine treats it as a signum-0 document that posts nothing, and its only downstream effect is to feed open-queue rows to the Consignment GRN when the company's knock-off configuration allows it.

No `JournalPostingTypeHandler` entry, so no journal, ARAP, tax or stock transactions on FINAL. ## How it connects

- **internal-consignment-grn-applet** — the GRN's KO tab is the consumer of this order's open queue; it copies `qty_open` and the GRN (signum +1 / −1) is where stock and the consignor liability first appear.
- **knock-off / organisation-applet** — the company Knock Off Configuration (`bl_fi_comp_gendoc_flow_config`) decides whether FINAL produces open-queue rows; it is the single most common reason "the GRN cannot find the order".
- **internal-purchase-order-applet** — same screens (the applet is a fork of the PO applet minus approvals, replenishment, intercompany and CLOSE); direction of the PO page's claims about open queue should be checked against the flow config too.
- **client-side-permissions** — with zero seeded definitions, price visibility depends on user rank, not on permission grants.
