## How it connects

- **cashbook** — the Weightage Configuration (bl_fi_mst_cashbook_recon_config) that drives auto-matching is edited on the Cashbook applet, not here; cashbook membership decides which cashbooks a user can reconcile.
- **internal-receipt-voucher-applet / internal-payment-voucher-applet** — their FINAL STL_MTHD lines are the cashbook side; once linked, amount edits are rejected server-side until unreconciled; VOID drops the line from the balance after the fact.
- **pos-general-applet** — POS settlements reach the same cashbook lines through settlement-method → cashbook mapping.
- **ledger-and-journal-applet** — manual journals do not create cashbook txn lines; bank charges must be vouchers or cash transfers to be reconcilable.
- **gl-posting** — bank reconciliation is outside the journal engine entirely; the only cross-effect is the reconciled-line edit guard.
