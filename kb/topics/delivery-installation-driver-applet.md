# Delivery And Installation Driver applet

The driver-facing half of the delivery module: a login linked to a driver record sees only its own trips, starts / completes / cancels the jobs on them, reorders stops, and records a proof of delivery (recipient name and IC, signature, photos, quantity delivered per line with a failure reason, cash collected). Every call goes to the backend's `login-driver-ep` endpoints, which check driver identity instead of permission sets.

## How it connects

- **delivery-installation-applet** — owns everything this applet consumes: driver record + login link, trips, jobs, `sequence_in_trip`, return reasons; driver actions appear there as job events and job-line proof data.
- **pick-pack-queue** — driver Cancel Job restores `qty_balance` exactly like the back-office cancel.
- **internal-sales-order-applet / internal-sales-invoice-applet / internal-delivery-order-applet** — driver Complete Job sets their `delivery_status` to PARTIALLY/FULLY DELIVERED.
- **driver-delivery-order-applet** — the other driver applet; different model (`delivery_driver_guid` on the DO), no proof of delivery.
- **return-reasons** — one `bl_applet_config` row shared by both delivery applets; the guid mismatch decides whether drivers see it.
- **applet-registry** — `documentation_url` points at Atlassian; should point at the wiki page.
