# Purchase GRN Stock In (Internal)

The receiving document that books goods into stock the moment they arrive: quantity signum +1, amount signum −1. FINAL creates inventory transactions, feeds the company moving-average cost, and posts Dr Inventory Not Invoiced / Dr Input Tax / Cr Creditor Not Invoiced. Its invoice leg is the Purchase Invoice No Stock In (0 / −1), which moves the liability to the trade creditor. It is the alternative to the plain Purchase GRN (0/0) + Purchase Invoice (+1/−1) pair; a company should use one pair, not both.
