# Stock take

A counting session for one location: devices are attached, records (item, quantity, UOM, serial / batch) are captured by scanning or typing, and a report is generated that puts the counted quantity beside the ledger's opening balance, movements during the count, closing balance and a Min–Max tolerance band. The stock take measures the gap; it never moves stock — an accepted variance becomes a manual Stock Adjustment.

## How it connects

- **stock-adjustment** — the only path from a counted variance to a ledger movement; the stock take stores adjustment columns nobody writes.
- **stock-balance / stock-availability / stock-report** — read the same `bl_inv_txn_line`; the report's EMP Quantity should equal the Stock Balance closing balance at the location for the same instant.
- **serial-numbers / batch-expiry** — the Scan Form validates serials against `sn_id` and batches against the batch header; `sys_bal_sn` comes from `bl_inv_txn_sub_line` with a positive `quantity_base` sum.
- **multi-uom / scan-code** — UOM barcodes decide whether a scan is recognised and what the recorded quantity is.
- **moving-average-cost / fifo-costing** — the report costs the variance at MA only.
- **workflow-design** — supplies the status/resolution values; nothing enforces them (open requests gh:bigledger/blg-intranet#4421, #4420).
- **applet-settings-storage** — the backend reads an applet setting from `bl_applet_ext` at close time, a pattern not seen in the document applets (where posting is fixed by signums).
- **mobile-stock-take** — shares tables and endpoints; most data-quality incidents originate there.
