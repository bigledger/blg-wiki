# Consignor Purchase Billing (Internal)

The period record of what you owe a consignor for consigned stock that became billable: per item an opening quantity, net purchases, closing quantity and the resulting Quantity To Bill with its value and tax. It is stored in its own tables, is not a generic document, posts no journal, moves no stock and creates no creditor balance. The real settlement still needs a purchase invoice and a payment voucher.

## How it connects

- **internal-consignment-grn-applet / internal-consignment-gin-applet** — the stock-moving consignment documents are the only source of the quantities typed on a billing line; the billing reads neither. The backend calc endpoint even looks at the *ordinary* purchase GRN/GIN types, not the consignment ones.
- **internal-consignment-purchase-order-applet** — its page already sends users here for consignor settlement; that pointer is wrong as shipped, because this applet cannot record a payment either.
- **internal-purchase-invoice-applet / internal-payment-voucher-applet** — the only documents that create and settle the creditor balance for a consignor.
- **journal-posting** — a consignment family member that posts nothing; the GRN/return journal (`PURCHASE_CONSIGNMENT` handler) is unaffected by billings.
- **client-side-permissions** — zero definitions and zero checks: a third pattern beside "checked but not seeded" (F-0044).

## Open questions

- Is the Payment tab meant to write `STL_MTHD` billing lines (the reducer expects them) or was payment recording abandoned in favour of PI + PV? The guard bug and the commented-out request assembly suggest the feature was never finished.
- Which formula is intended for Quantity To Bill — the UI's opening + net − closing, or the backend's opening + net + closing? And should net purchase count consignment GRN/GIN rather than ordinary GRN/GIN?
- Should SAVE persist line changes (re-enable the commented `bl_fi_csg_billing_line = [...pns, ...stl]`)?
