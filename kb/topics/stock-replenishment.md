# Stock Replenishment

Templates (scope + fulfillment strategy), events (calendar, optionally recurring) and runs (one execution) that compare each item × location against its minimum or maximum level on the location stock balance and produce order-quantity lines, fulfillment lines and — through the generic document service — purchase orders or outbound stock transfers. The applet itself moves no stock and posts no journal.

## How it connects

- **internal-purchase-order-applet** — generated `INTERNAL_PURCHASE_ORDER` (DRAFT or FINAL by *Document Status*), lines merged per branch + supplier, UOM `PCS`, no GL / tax; the PO applet's own *PO Replenishment* menu is a separate older screen family and should point here.
- **stock-transfer** — generated `INTERNAL_OUTBOUND_STOCK_TRANSFER` for internal, surplus and inter-company lines; FINAL status moves stock immediately; the Stock Transfer page's "replenishment runs generate outbound transfers" is now true (issue #20, July 2026).
- **intercompany** — inter-company transfer needs the branch pairing OUTBOUND → INBOUND stock transfer (Organisation v3) and GL mapping; AUTO mode creates the inbound leg.
- **inv-item-maintenance / stock-balance** — `qty_min_level` / `qty_max_level` on the location balance row are the only inputs to the formula; the Min/Max tab edits the same row.
- **doc-item-maintenance** — Entity Pricing `supplier_priority`, `purchase_unit_price`, `purchase_qty_min_order` / `max_order`, `purchase_order_lead_time_day` drive supplier choice and PO price.
- **supplier-maintenance** — inactive or non-supplier entities are dropped from candidate links.
- **recurring-events** — same RRULE materialisation pattern (100-instance cap, THIS / FOLLOWING / ALL update types) as the consolidation and auto-top-up event services.
- **email-notification** — applet notification templates (`bl_applet_notification_template_hdr`) with `descr` as subject.
