# Purchase Invoice No Stock In (Internal)

The supplier invoice that follows a Purchase GRN Stock In. Quantity signum 0, amount signum −1: it never creates an inventory transaction. FINAL books Dr Purchase / Dr Input Tax / Cr Creditor and, whenever the invoice carries a knock-off link, a second pair Dr Creditor Not Invoiced / Cr Inventory Not Invoiced that reverses the accrual the GRN Stock In raised. The old page's premise ("an invoice for services and overheads") was wrong; it is the invoice leg of the GRN Stock In + PI No Stock In receiving pair.

## How it connects

- **internal-purchase-grn-stock-in-applet** — the receipt leg; its FINAL raises the accrual this invoice reverses, and its open-queue row auto-creates this document.
- **internal-purchase-invoice-applet** — the stock-moving alternative; raising it for goods already on a GRN Stock In double-counts stock (intranet #3940), and it is the only purchase invoice type the self-billed e-Invoice pipeline recognises.
- **gendoc-flow-config / knock-off** — every KO tab and the auto-creation depend on `bl_fi_comp_gendoc_flow_config` rows; `doc_2_posting_status` decides DRAFT vs FINAL of the auto-created invoice.
- **default-gl-codes** — needs `CREDITOR` (or `CREDITOR_NON_TRADE`), `PURCHASE`, `INPUT_TAX`, `CREDITOR_NOT_INVOICED`, `INVENTORY_NOT_INVOICED`.
- **e-invoice-self-billed** — the applet exposes the action; the backend pipeline does not list the type.
- **internal-purchase-return-applet / internal-payment-voucher-applet** — downstream; a return link blocks VOID client-side, any target link blocks it server-side.

## Open questions

- Should the accrual-reversal leg use the net amount (matching the GRN Stock In's Inventory Not Invoiced debit) instead of net + tax? Today the pair never clears exactly when tax is captured.
- Should the reversal leg be restricted to links whose source is `INTERNAL_PURCHASE_GRN_STOCK_IN`? Today a PO-only knock-off also moves the accrual accounts.
- Will `INTERNAL_PURCHASE_INVOICE_NO_STOCK_IN` be added to the self-billed e-Invoice pipeline (applet #18)? If not, the SELF-BILLED button and E-Invoice tab should be hidden here.
