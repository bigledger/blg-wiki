# Purchase GRN (Internal)

The goods-received record. Amount and quantity signum are both 0, so FINAL writes no inventory transaction and no journal; it only opens the lines in the open-queue for the purchase invoice, which is the document that books stock in and the creditor. The stock-moving receipt is a different applet (Purchase GRN Stock In, +1 / −1), paired with Purchase Invoice No Stock In (0 / −1).
