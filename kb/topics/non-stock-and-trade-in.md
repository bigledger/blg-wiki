# Non Stock and Trade In (NSTI)

A per-unit register (`bl_fi_nsti_stock_hdr`) of traded-in and non-stock items — one row per item + serial number + location with a running `qty_base` — kept outside the inventory ledger. Units are mostly created and moved by FINAL documents (POS trade-in lines, sales lines, credit notes) through two queue processors; the applet is the place to look them up, correct them and attach files. Categories and a CSV import (staging only) live in the same applet.

## How it connects

- **doc-item-maintenance** — the item type `NSTI` is what the processors key on; the record's Financial Item picker is unfiltered.
- **pos-general-applet** — `SHOW_TRADE_IN` / `TRADE_IN_BASIC_ITEM`; the cash bill's trade-in line (`INTERNAL_PURCHASE_TRADE_IN`) is the usual origin of a unit; a later sale disposes of it; location on the line is part of the unit's identity.
- **internal-sales-invoice-applet / internal-sales-credit-note-applet** — dispose / restore serials; credit note forced +1.
- **stock-transfer** — `createStockInTransitTxns` implies NSTI units move on transfers (negated counter-lines); design discussed in gt#5853; not documented on the transfer page.
- **stock-availability** — the only stock view that includes NSTI; **stock-balance / stock-report** exclude it.
- **chart-of-accounts / financial-report-applet** — `NSTI`, `COGS_NSTI`, `STOCK_BALANCE_NSTI`; closing-stock NSTI value; `SHOW_NSTI_STOCK_VALUE`.
- **organisation** — company/branch/location; location is in the balance key.
- **tax-configuration** — tax/WHT codes on manual lines. **employee-applet** — person in charge.
