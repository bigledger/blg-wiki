# Shipping Pricebook applet (shipping price books and price sets)

A shipping price book is a container of shipping price sets; each price set is a `bl_fi_mst_shipping_price_book_line` with rules (header, multi-line, single-line) in `conditional_filter_json` and up to three fee treatments (Standard Shipping Fee, Handling Fee, Fuel Surcharge) in `treatment_json`. The applet only maintains the master data. The only consumer is the CP Commerce storefront: at checkout the shopper picks a shipping price book as the "Shipping method", the storefront evaluates it client-side and writes the resulting fee as a line for the website's shipping-fee service item.

## How it connects

- **pricebook-applet** — same rule / treatment design and the same two-editor layout (menu Price Set vs nested Pricing Set), on separate tables; the Pricebook consumers (POS, sales documents) never read shipping price books.
- **cp-commerce-admin-applet** — owns the website switches that decide whether, and with which price book and service item, the storefront charges shipping.
- **shopping-cart-applet** — the shipping fee is a line of the DRAFT cart; the coupon engine may discount it (free-shipping label).
- **doc-item-maintenance-applet** — the shipping-fee SERVICE item and the pricing schemes offered in the Treatment tab; item weight / dimensions feed the weight bands.
- **organisation-applet** — companies and branches for Company / Branch rules.
- **membership-admin-applet** — member classes and labels for the member rules.
- **internal-sales-order-applet** — the cart converts to a sales order that carries the shipping line as a normal service line.
