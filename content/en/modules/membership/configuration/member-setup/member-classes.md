Member classes define the tier hierarchy in your loyalty program — Bronze, Silver, Gold, or any structure that fits your business. A member belongs to exactly one class at a time. The class controls which pricing, earning rules, and benefits apply.

> [!TIP]
> Before creating classes, align with your marketing team on how many tiers you need and what benefit differentiates each one. Adding classes later is easy — restructuring an existing tier hierarchy with live members is not.
