Member labels are flexible tags you apply to members for segmentation — things like VIP, Newsletter, High-Value, or Seasonal. Unlike classes, a member can hold multiple labels at the same time, and labels do not affect pricing or tier benefits.

> [!TIP]
> Use labels for marketing segmentation and campaign targeting. Use classes for benefit tiers. Never create a class when a label would do — it overcomplicates the tier structure.
