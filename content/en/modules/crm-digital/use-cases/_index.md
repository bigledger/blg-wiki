The Digital CRM Module flexibly supports diverse customer engagement workflows across contact centers, automated marketing, and event management.

## 1. Omnichannel Customer Support Center (WhatsApp & Live Chat)

**Business Scenario:** A retail merchant operates a central support team handling customer inquiries across WhatsApp Business, web live chat, and email from a single unified workspace.

- **Primary Applets:** [Unified Contact Center UCC Applet](/applets/crm/unified-contact-center-ucc-applet/), [Customer Maintenance Applet](/applets/master-data/customer-maintenance-applet/)
- **Operational Flow:** Customer messages via WhatsApp → UCC applet routes conversation to available support agent → agent views past purchase history and resolves ticket instantly.

---

## 2. Automated Post-Purchase Lead Nurturing (Drip Campaigns)

**Business Scenario:** An e-commerce brand automatically sends educational product tips and cross-sell promotional vouchers 7 days after a customer completes a web purchase.

- **Primary Applets:** [Engagement Applet](/applets/crm/engagement-applet/), [Voucher Management Applet](/applets/membership/voucher-management-applet/)
- **Operational Flow:** Web order completed in E-Commerce → Engagement applet triggers 7-day delay → automated personalized email sent with a unique discount voucher code.
