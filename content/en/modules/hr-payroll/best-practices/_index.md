Follow these operational best practices to maintain payroll computation accuracy, ensure timely statutory tax submissions, and protect employee salary confidentiality.

## 1. Attendance & Overtime Cut-Off Verification
- **Enforce Monthly Time Log Cut-Offs:** Lock monthly time attendance logs in the [Admin Time Attendance Applet](/applets/human-resources/admin-time-attendance-applet/) 5 business days prior to payroll processing to prevent post-computation attendance modifications.
- **Biometric Anti-Buddy Punching:** Require biometric thumbprint or facial recognition scanning at factory gates to eliminate fraudulent buddy-clocking.

## 2. Statutory Compliance & Filing Timelines
- **Strict Statutory Deadlines:** Submit monthly EPF, SOCSO, EIS, and PCB tax payments prior to government monthly deadlines (e.g., 15th of each month) to avoid statutory late-payment interest fines.
- **Encrypted Payslip Distribution:** Password-protect all digital PDF payslips sent to employees to maintain data privacy compliance.
