The Sales & POS Module provides rich multi-dimensional reporting tools enabling store supervisors, sales managers, and finance teams to analyze revenue performance, audit cash drawers, and track profitability.

## Reporting Scenario Decision Matrix ("Which Report to Use When")

Select the appropriate report based on your specific operational or business decision scenario:

| Business Scenario / Question | Recommended Report | Primary Applet | Key Metrics & Decision Value |
|------------------------------|--------------------|----------------|------------------------------|
| **"Did the cashier's physical drawer match system sales at shift end?"** | Daily Cashier Reconciliation Report | [Daily Cashier Report Applet](/applets/sales-workflow/daily-cashier-report-applet/) | Opening float, cash sales, card settlement, drawer overage/shortage balance. |
| **"Which sales reps are generating the highest gross profit margin (GP%)?"** | Salesperson Profitability & Margin Analysis | [Sales Report Applet](/applets/sales-workflow/sales-report-applet/) | Revenue by salesman, cost of sales (COGS), gross profit dollar amount, GP margin %. |
| **"What are our top-selling product SKUs across all retail outlets?"** | Product Ranking & Item Sales Analysis | [Sales Report Applet](/applets/sales-workflow/sales-report-applet/) | Quantity sold, total sales value, top 20 revenue-generating SKUs by store branch. |
| **"How does store performance compare across different branches?"** | Multi-Branch Revenue Comparison | [Sales Report Applet](/applets/sales-workflow/sales-report-applet/) | Store revenue totals, transaction counts, average basket size per retail counter. |
| **"How much sales revenue was generated for specific consignment suppliers?"** | Supplier Sales Access Report | [Sales Report Supplier Access Applet](/applets/sales-workflow/sales-report-supplier-access-applet/) | Vendor-specific sales volume, consigned goods turnover, and consignment billing basis. |

## Key Report Categories

### 1. Cashier & Shift Auditing
Designed for retail store supervisors to audit daily counter operations:
- **Shift Z-Reports:** Final end-of-day summary of transactions, voided sales, and cash drawer counts.
- **Payment Method Breakdown:** Detailed totals separated by Cash, Credit Card, E-Wallet, and Voucher Redemptions.

### 2. Commercial Sales & Margin Analytics
Designed for sales executives and managers to monitor performance against targets:
- **Sales by Customer:** Identifies key account revenue, transaction history, and credit term compliance.
- **Sales by Document Type:** Compares quotation conversion rates against confirmed sales orders and billing invoices.
