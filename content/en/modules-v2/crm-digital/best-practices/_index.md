Follow these operational best practices to maintain high customer satisfaction (CSAT), prevent messaging spam penalties, and optimize agent productivity.

## 1. Contact Center SLA & Agent Response Routing
- **Enforce First-Response SLAs:** Configure automated ticket routing rules in the [Unified Contact Center UCC Applet](/applets/crm/unified-contact-center-ucc-applet/) to ensure all incoming WhatsApp and Live Chat inquiries receive an initial response within 15 minutes.
- **Agent Collision Prevention:** Utilize active conversation locks to prevent multiple support agents from replying to the same customer simultaneously.

## 2. Broadcast Frequency Capping & Opt-Outs
- **Message Frequency Caps:** Limit automated marketing broadcast messages in the [Engagement Applet](/applets/crm/engagement-applet/) to a maximum of 2 messages per week per customer to prevent unsubscribe fatigue.
- **Mandatory Opt-Out Links:** Ensure every WhatsApp or email broadcast contains an automated opt-out link to comply with international anti-spam regulations.
