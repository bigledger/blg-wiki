Understanding core CRM concepts is essential before configuring messaging gateways or launching broadcast campaigns. These concepts explain **how customer interactions across multiple channels unify into a single customer profile** and how AI insights drive automated retention.

## Omnichannel Customer Lifecycle & Routing Flow

Customer support tickets and automated marketing journeys follow a unified 5-step lifecycle pipeline:

![Digital CRM Omnichannel Lifecycle Pipeline](/images/crm-digital/crm_lifecycle.png)

| Step | Milestone | Business Purpose | Applet Used |
|------|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| **1** | **Inquiry Capture** | Multi-channel message received via WhatsApp, Live Chat, or Email | [Unified Contact Center UCC Applet](/applets/crm/unified-contact-center-ucc-applet/) |
| **2** | **Unified Routing** | Ticket assigned to available support agent based on skills and workload | [Unified Contact Center UCC Applet](/applets/crm/unified-contact-center-ucc-applet/) |
| **3** | **Agent Resolution** | Support agent views full customer order history and resolves inquiry | [Unified Contact Center UCC Applet](/applets/crm/unified-contact-center-ucc-applet/) |
| **4** | **AI Sentiment Log** | Natural Language Processing scores satisfaction and updates RFM profile | [AI Customer Analytics Applet](/applets/crm/ai-customer-analytics-applet/) |
| **5** | **Drip Nurture** | Automated follow-up voucher sequence or feedback campaign triggered | [Engagement Applet](/applets/crm/engagement-applet/) |
