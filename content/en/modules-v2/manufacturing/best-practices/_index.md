Follow these operational best practices to maintain production schedule reliability, minimize material waste, and ensure accurate manufacturing cost absorption.

## 1. Real-Time Shop Floor Material Issuance
- **Enforce Immediate WIP Issuance:** Never issue raw materials verbally on the factory floor without logging the material transfer in the [Manufacturing Operation Applet](/applets/manufacturing/manufacturing-operation-applet/).
- **Log Component Scrap Immediately:** Record raw material damage or trimming waste during operations to maintain accurate inventory balances.

## 2. Work Center Labor & Overhead Absorption
- **Daily Machine Hour Audits:** Ensure machine operators log actual operational run hours and downtime reasons daily to prevent overhead absorption variances at month-end.
- **Regular BOM Audits:** Reconcile standard Bill of Materials component quantities against actual shop floor consumption quarterly.
