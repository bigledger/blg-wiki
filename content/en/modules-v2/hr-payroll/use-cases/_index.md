The HR & Payroll Module flexibly supports diverse workforce models across shift-based factories, hourly retail networks, and salaried corporate enterprises.

## 1. Multi-Shift Manufacturing Plant (Roster & Overtime Management)

**Business Scenario:** A 24/7 manufacturing plant operates 3 rotating shifts with strict overtime approval requirements and attendance tracking across 500 factory workers.

- **Primary Applets:** [Admin Time Attendance Applet](/applets/human-resources/admin-time-attendance-applet/), Payroll Engine
- **Operational Flow:** Workers clock in at biometric turnstiles → shift supervisor verifies overtime hours → attendance logs auto-populate monthly payroll → overtime multipliers calculated for payout.

---

## 2. Retail Store Network (Part-Time & Hourly Shift Schedules)

**Business Scenario:** A retail chain employs part-time store assistants working flexible hourly schedules across multiple store branches.

- **Primary Applets:** [Admin Time Attendance Applet](/applets/human-resources/admin-time-attendance-applet/), [Organisation Applet](/applets/master-data/organisation-applet/)
- **Operational Flow:** Store manager publishes weekly shift roster → staff clock in via mobile GPS app → hourly wages calculated based on verified attendance logs.
