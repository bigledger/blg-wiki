Cash conversion is the active redemption mechanism — when a member chooses to use their points at the counter, this rule determines how much monetary value their balance converts to. This is distinct from earning rules (which add points after a sale) and from tier pricing (which changes item prices without consuming points).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Cash conversion at POS requires both an active points currency **and** an active conversion rate configured. If either is missing or inactive, the cashier will not be able to process a redemption.
