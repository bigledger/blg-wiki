Follow these operational best practices to prevent duplicate expense claims, enforce corporate policy compliance, and maintain tax audit readiness.

## 1. Mandatory Digital Receipt Auditing
- **Enforce OCR Receipt Matching:** Require original digital receipt uploads for all claims exceeding policy minimums (e.g., $10.00) in the [Claim Applet](/applets/claims/claim-applet/).
- **Automated Duplicate Receipt Detection:** Enable system flags that alert finance whenever identical receipt amounts, dates, or tax invoice numbers are submitted twice.

## 2. Strict Entitlement & Cut-Off Cycles
- **Enforce Monthly Claim Cut-Off Dates:** Establish strict monthly submission windows in the [Claim Cycle Applet](/applets/claims/claim-cycle-applet/) (e.g., 25th of every month) to ensure expense accruals align with financial accounting periods.
- **Clear Policy Exception Sign-Offs:** Require explicit secondary manager approval for any claims exceeding standard grade entitlement caps.
