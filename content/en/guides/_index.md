## 📖 Learning Paths

Follow these structured sequences to master each area. Each path links to existing guides in recommended order.

### 💰 Finance Path

| # | Guide | What You'll Learn |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [Chart of Accounts Setup](/guides/accounting-guides/chart-of-accounts-setup/) | Design your account structure |
| 2 | [Journal Entries](/guides/accounting-guides/journal-entries/) | Record financial transactions |
| 3 | [Bank Reconciliation](/guides/accounting-guides/bank-reconciliation-guide/) | Match bank statements to records |
| 4 | [Financial Reporting](/guides/accounting-guides/financial-reporting/) | Generate standard financial statements |
| 5 | Financial Reporting Excellence | Advanced analytics & custom reports |
| 6 | Compliance & Audit | Regulatory compliance & audit prep |

### 💼 Sales Path

| # | Guide | What You'll Learn |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [Standard Sales Workflow](/guides/sales-guides/standard-sales-workflow/) | Complete order-to-delivery flow |
| 2 | [Credit Sales](/guides/sales-guides/credit-sales-workflow/) | Selling on credit terms |
| 3 | [Cash Sales](/guides/sales-guides/cash-sales-workflow/) | Walk-in immediate payment sales |
| 4 | [Instalment Sales](/guides/sales-guides/instalment-sales-workflow/) | Financing agreements (AEON, JCL) |
| 5 | [Partial Delivery](/guides/sales-guides/partial-delivery-workflow/) | Split shipments & multiple deliveries |
| 6 | [Installation Scheduling](/guides/sales-guides/installation-scheduling-workflow/) | Appliances requiring installation |
| 7 | [Returns & Exchanges](/guides/sales-guides/returns-exchanges-workflow/) | Post-sale returns handling |

### 🛒 Procurement Path

| # | Guide | What You'll Learn |
|---|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | [Standard Procurement](/guides/purchasing-guides/standard-procurement-workflow/) | Full requisition-to-payment cycle |
| 2 | [Direct GRN Workflow](/guides/purchasing-guides/direct-grn-workflow/) | Receive goods without PO |
| 3 | [Direct Invoice Workflow](/guides/purchasing-guides/direct-invoice-workflow/) | Invoice-driven purchasing |
| 4 | [Invoice-First Workflow](/guides/purchasing-guides/invoice-first-workflow/) | Process invoice before goods receipt |
| 5 | [Consignment Purchasing](/guides/purchasing-guides/consignment-purchasing/) | Vendor-managed inventory |
