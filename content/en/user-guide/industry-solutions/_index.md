## The BigLedger Industry Advantage

Traditional ERP systems force businesses into rigid, one-size-fits-all software packages. BigLedger takes a **modular Applet-based approach**:

1. **Core Foundation** — Shared master data (Chart of Accounts, Tax Config, Cashbooks, Customers, Suppliers).
2. **Specialized Applets** — Pick and combine only the specific operational tools your industry requires.
3. **Unified Operational Data Lake** — All applets sync seamlessly in real time with zero duplicate data entry.
