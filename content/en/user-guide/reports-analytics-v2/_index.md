## Master Reports Catalog

BigLedger provides pre-built reports organized by business function to support operational monitoring and financial compliance:

| Category | Report | Applet / Location | Key Decision It Drives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Financials** | P&L (Income Statement) | [Financial Report Applet](/applets/finance/financial-report-applet/) | Profitability analysis, operating margins, budget variance |
| **Statutory Financials** | Balance Sheet | [Financial Report Applet](/applets/finance/financial-report-applet/) | Financial position, asset & liability tracking, audit tie-out |
| **Statutory Financials** | Trial Balance | [Financial Report Applet](/applets/finance/financial-report-applet/) | Verification that total debits equal total credits before close |
| **Payables & Cashflow** | Creditor Aging (AP) | [Creditor Report Applet](/applets/finance/creditor-report-applet/) | Cash outflow planning, supplier payment prioritization |
| **Receivables & Debtors**| Debtor Aging (AR) | [Debtor Report Applet](/applets/finance/debtor-report-applet/) | Collection pipeline management, chasing overdue accounts |
| **Receivables & Debtors**| Statement of Account (SOA) | [Statement of Account Applet](/applets/finance/statement-of-account-applet/) | Customer reconciliation statements and dispute resolution |
| **Ledger Audit** | GL Account Inquiry | [General Ledger Applet](/applets/finance/general-ledger-applet/) | Subledger drill-down to trace any GL line to source document |
| **Retail & POS** | Cashier Daily Settlement | POS General Applet | Matching cashier shift takings with ledger bank accounts |
