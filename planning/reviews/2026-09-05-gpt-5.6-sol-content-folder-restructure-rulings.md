# GPT-5.6-sol rulings on the four contested IA points

## Point 1

**RULING — Use `how-to/` and `processes/`.**

`tasks/` does not tell readers that it contains instructions, and “one document, one screen” is an editorial rule they cannot predict. `workflows/` is worse because BigLedger already uses Workflow as a product and applet term. `how-to/` clearly promises a focused outcome; `processes/` clearly signals a longer journey across applets and modules. Display them as “How-to guides” and “Business processes.”

## Point 2

**RULING — Use short, plain-English slugs; use fuller plain-English titles followed by the ERP term in parentheses.**

Use `customer-orders-to-payment/`, `supplier-orders-to-payment/`, and `closing-the-books/`. These are shorter than the reviewer’s sentence-like slugs without collapsing back into module names such as Sales or Purchasing. Titles should be “Sell, deliver and get paid (order to cash),” “Buy, receive and pay suppliers (procure to pay),” and “Close the books (record to report).” Hugo aliases for `order-to-cash`, `procure-to-pay`, and `record-to-report` are worthwhile: they preserve familiar, searchable ERP terminology without making jargon the primary navigation language.

## Point 3

**RULING — Put them at `user-guide/roles/` and `user-guide/industries/`.**

Reject `views/`: it describes the IA mechanism, not anything a Malaysian SME reader would seek. Also reject nesting them under `getting-started/`; a finance manager or retailer will revisit these hubs long after onboarding, and industry pathways cover ongoing operations as well as setup. “Roles” and “Industries” are direct, predictable labels, while the User Guide umbrella supplies their context.

## Point 4

**RULING — Put all reader-facing sections under one canonical `user-guide/` umbrella.**

Seven root-level reader sections would overload the sidebar and blur the existing top-bar distinction between guidance, Modules, Applets, and Developers. The previous problem was not the existence of an umbrella; it was having two indistinguishable ones, `guides/` and `user-guide/`. Keep exactly one—`user-guide/`, matching the visible top-menu label—and retire the competing umbrella through redirects or aliases.

## Recommended tree

```text
content/en/
├── user-guide/                         # Sole home for reader-facing guidance
│   ├── getting-started/                # First login, orientation, first success
│   ├── setup/                          # Initial business and system configuration
│   ├── how-to/                         # Focused guides for completing one outcome
│   ├── processes/                      # Cross-module, end-to-end business journeys
│   │   ├── customer-orders-to-payment/ # Sell, deliver and get paid
│   │   ├── supplier-orders-to-payment/ # Buy, receive and pay suppliers
│   │   └── closing-the-books/          # Complete period-end reporting
│   ├── roles/                          # Hub pages linking guidance by job role
│   ├── industries/                     # Hub pages linking guidance by industry
│   └── training/                       # Structured courses and practice material
├── modules/                            # Business groupings that link to applets
├── applets/                            # Canonical applet reference documentation
├── developers/                         # APIs, integrations, and extension guidance
└── support/                            # Troubleshooting and support routes
```

## Where I disagree with the first reviewer

- `views/` should not appear in a public URL.
- Reader-facing sections should not be root-level siblings.
- The IA should have five principal roots, not ten.
- Process slugs should be shorter than the reviewer’s sentence-like versions while remaining plain English.
