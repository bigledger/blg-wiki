> Claude drafts and reconciles; GPT attacks. Claims below are GPT's and are NOT yet verified.

The section index is a landing page rather than a single task guide, so the mandatory guide skeleton should not be applied mechanically. Its concise lifecycle explanation and operational grouping are good.

## 5. Ordering

The numeric weights are internally sensible: 1, 10, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45. The rendered sequence should therefore be requirements → setup → routing → validation → month end → correction → PEPPOL.

The index cards are not sufficient for a first-time reader:

- “Read these three, in this order” includes requirements, setup, and routing only ([_index.md:27-35](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/guides/einvoice-guides/_index.md:27)).
- Validation is filed only under “When something goes wrong,” even though setup depends on its address/identity rules and its override behaviour.
- There is no explicit controlled-pilot or daily-operation page between setup and month end.
- Incoming supplier and self-billed work is not represented.
- PEPPOL is correctly described as optional, but its card/link should be removed until the page is trustworthy.

A safer first-time path would be:

1. Malaysia requirements.
2. Roles, access, and master-data readiness.
3. MyInvois intermediary appointment.
4. BigLedger company configuration and routing defaults.
5. Controlled live/test pilot with validation recovery.
6. Daily outgoing, incoming, and self-billed operations.
7. Pools and exception handling.
8. Month-end plus genuine LHDN reconciliation.
9. Cancellation/credit-note incidents.
10. Optional PEPPOL.

## 6. The AWS comparison

Three implementable practices are missing:

1. **Exact “Before you begin” access and environment blocks.** AWS tells readers which account, role, permission, region, and resource names they need before Step 1. Tomorrow, BigLedger could add the exact MyInvois role, permission checkbox names, intermediary identity, BigLedger permission set, legal entity, test/live environment, and expected confirmation screen.

For example: company code, document number, queue, status, request error, validation time, and screenshots—with explicit warnings not to include customer identity data.
