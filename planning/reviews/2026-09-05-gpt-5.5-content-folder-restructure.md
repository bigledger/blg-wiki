**2. `tasks/` vs `workflows/`**
No. A first-time reader cannot predict it. “Task” sounds like anything they need to do; “workflow” sounds like internal ERP jargon and BigLedger already has “Workflow” applets.

Use:

- `how-to/` = one operational outcome, usually one document or one setup action.
- `processes/` = end-to-end business process across documents, roles, or modules.

Examples:

- `/how-to/create-sales-order/`
- `/how-to/create-purchase-order/`
- `/how-to/set-up-teams-and-permissions/`
- `/processes/sell-deliver-and-get-paid/`
- `/processes/buy-receive-and-pay/`
- `/processes/close-the-books/`

**3. ERP Jargon Slugs**
Case for `order-to-cash`, `procure-to-pay`, `record-to-report`: consultants, finance managers, and ERP searchers know them. They are compact and standard.

Case against: BigLedger’s writing rules explicitly reject SAP-style language. A cashier, warehouse clerk, or SME owner will not think “I need order-to-cash”. They think “sell goods and get paid”.

Pick:

- URL slug: plain English.
- Page title: plain English.
- Add jargon as `aliases`, tags, and first-paragraph parenthetical.

Recommended:

- `/processes/sell-deliver-and-get-paid/` — title: “Sell, Deliver, and Get Paid”
- `/processes/buy-receive-and-pay-suppliers/` — title: “Buy, Receive, and Pay Suppliers”
- `/processes/close-the-books/` — title: “Close the Books and Report Your Numbers”

Use aliases like `/workflows/order-to-cash/` if SEO matters.

**4. `stock-movement`**
Weak name. It is a bucket of leftovers, not a process.

Dissolve it unless you can define an end-to-end reader outcome. Split into:

- `/how-to/transfer-stock-between-branches/`
- `/how-to/adjust-stock-after-a-count/`
- `/how-to/run-a-stock-take/`
- `/processes/replenish-branch-stock/` if there is a real chain from low stock to requisition to transfer/receiving.

[stock-transfer.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/guides/inventory-guides/stock-transfer.md:11) is not enough to justify a top-level process family.

**5. `implementation/`**
It earns its keep, but only if it means “go-live setup before daily use”, not “any configuration page”.

Keep it top-level for cross-module setup:

- chart of accounts
- company/branch/location
- users/roles/permissions baseline
- tax/SST/e-invoice setup
- cashbooks
- item master/pricing foundations

Do not bury [chart-of-accounts-setup.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/guides/accounting-guides/chart-of-accounts-setup.md:11) inside `close-the-books`; the user needs it before sales invoices, purchases, bank reconciliation, and reporting. It is infrastructure.

Cut/nest:

- Keep `modules/`, `applets/`, `developers/` top-level.

**7. Roles, Industries, Reports, Administration**
- `roles/`: should be a hub/view, not an owning taxonomy. Role pages should link to setup, how-to, process, and report pages. Current role pages like [warehouse-manager.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/guides/roles/warehouse-manager.md:18) are cross-cutting scenario maps.
- `industries/`: hub/view. [retail-and-ecommerce.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/user-guide/industry-solutions/retail-and-ecommerce.md:79) recommends applet stacks; it should link out, not own duplicate guidance.
- `reports/`: mostly a task/how-to view. “Run Trial Balance” can live under `how-to/`; “month-end reporting pack” belongs under `processes/close-the-books/`; module report catalogs belong in module pages.
- `administration/`: real section, but reader is sysadmin. Keep as `admin/` or `administration/`, under setup/help, not mixed with ordinary daily operations.

The proposal misses or mishandles:

- `applications/`: UCC is not covered. Either nest into `modules/customer-communications/` or keep `applications/` for productized apps that are bigger than applets.
- `tutorials/`: not mentioned. Fold into `getting-started/` or `how-to/`.

**9. Hugo/Hextra Mechanics**
Bad news if this is done casually.

- Every moved page needs `aliases`. Only one file currently uses `aliases`, [creditor-report-applet.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/applets/finance/creditor-report-applet.md:11). A URL reset without aliases will break almost all inbound links.
- `hugo.yaml` main menu already has broken `pageRef`s: `/modules-v2/sales-crm`, `/modules-v2/erp`, `/modules-v2/it-ce`, `/modules-v2/service-industry`, `/modules-v2/drop-shipping` are referenced in [hugo.yaml](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/hugo.yaml:169) but missing.
- Hextra requires `_index.md` for section behavior. Current repo has them; the restructure must preserve that.
- Section weights will need a full pass. Otherwise Hextra sidebars will look random after moving mixed old/new content.
- Inline JS redirects exist, e.g. [user-guide/_index.md](/home/marketing/repos/blg-wiki/content/en/user-guide/_index.md:10). Replace with aliases or proper redirects. Content-level JS redirects are brittle.
- Custom inline HTML/CSS in industry and `*-v2` pages violates CLAUDE.md’s Hextra guidance and will not age well through a restructure.

2. Use plain-English process slugs and titles; keep ERP jargon only as aliases/tags.
3. Make `roles/`, `industries/`, and most `reports/` hub views, not owning folders that duplicate canonical pages.
